Hi my dear Saba, haha. I’m building this site little by little on my phone, and my leg is broken. You came over last night and we had such a good time. We slept next to each other, haha. I love you so much. I called you to come see this, but you were asleep. Today is an odd-numbered day, and you have Abbasi’s language class around 6. I love you so, so much. You are my heart.
